FIG. 02 — SEQUENCE

The five stops between "curious" and "hired"

Skip a stop and the next one gets harder. Take them in order and each one gets easier than the last.

01

Lay the foundation

Kotlin syntax, the Android component lifecycle, and enough Android Studio fluency that the tool stops fighting you.

02

Ship small, ugly things

A notes app. A calculator. A quiz that pulls questions from an API. The point isn't polish — it's finishing.

Open the 2026 roadmap breakdown ↗
03

Turn projects into a portfolio

Three working apps on GitHub with clear write-ups beat a folder of certificates, every single time.

04

Get real feedback pressure

An internship, a freelance gig, even an unpaid favor for a local business — anything with a deadline and a stakeholder.

05

Apply on purpose

Target roles that match your portfolio's strengths, not just any listing with "Android" in the title.
